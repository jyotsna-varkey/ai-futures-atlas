AI Futures Atlas

What decides which world AI builds?

Fourteen open questions separate one future from another. This atlas maps them, shows how they shape each other, and paints the worlds they lead to. It is a framework for thinking rather than a forecast, so it assigns no probabilities. The aim is to argue about the right things and to notice early which path we are on.

Going deeper

The map is a scaffold. These methods fill it with evidence and with other people’s judgment, so the framework sharpens without us having to pick answers.

The camps behind the map

Serious thinkers about AI futures mostly share the same frameworks and disagree on a handful of assumptions. Each world on this map is roughly one camp’s assumptions played forward, so the camps are the quickest way to see where an argument comes from. The worlds were written from these camps; they have not yet been derived from a model.
